Install and update Android apps straight from where their developers publish them. Tern checks every file before Android sees it.

Download Tern All releases

For Android 10 and later, on phones, tablets and TVs. About 5 MB. Each release lists the SHA-256 of its file.

What it does

  • Follows the releases of apps on GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg and other Forgejo servers, SourceHut, SourceForge, Jenkins, F-Droid repositories and plain download links, and in the stores Obtainium reads, from APKPure to the Galaxy Store.
  • Checks who signed each file, holds every update to the certificate of the first install, and compares the checksum the publisher gave. A file that fails is not installed.
  • Says what it found in plain words, and keeps a log of every download, check and install.
  • Installs without asking through Shizuku or root if you like, and shows what is waiting on a widget, a Quick Settings tile and in notifications you can update from.
  • Works by remote on Android TV. A phone can send links and your app list to the TV, sealed with a code that never leaves the screen.
  • Goes through Tor with Orbot when you ask it to, and never around it.
  • Reads an export from Obtainium, so your list comes along.
  • Needs no account, and has no analytics and no ads. It is free software under the GPL.

Put Tern on your app's page

A badge in your README lets people add your app to Tern with one tap. Paste the address of your app's releases, then copy the Markdown.
